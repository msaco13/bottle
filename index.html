Keep Bottle on your iPhone

Put it on your Home Screen. Your bottles come with you, and it can tell you when a new one arrives (an iPhone only allows that from the Home Screen). Three taps:

  1. Tap the Share button in the bar at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  3. Open Bottle from your Home Screen and tap Turn on notifications.

How Bottle works

  1. Write a note. Up to 600 characters. Add a picture or a GIF if you like.
  2. Send it in one tap. Your messages open with the bottle already in place. Nothing to copy or paste.
  3. They open it. A bottle washes up on a beach with your note inside.
  4. They write back. One button under your note, and their bottle comes back to you.
  5. Skip the texting. Once you both turn on notifications, bottles go straight from phone to phone.

No app to download and no account. A bottle opens for whoever holds its link, so send it to the person it is for.
